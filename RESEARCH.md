## Synthesis → what People.Media will do
1. **Concept**: People.Media = "the people-powered media network" — human stories, people profiles, creator spotlights, and an annual People.Media 100 list. Combines HONY/Upworthy storytelling (traffic), Famous Birthdays/Biography people pages (search traffic), TIME100/Forbes 30U30 nominations (lead capture), and Entrepreneur/Fast Company paid features & awards (high-ticket revenue).
2. **Revenue stack** (ranked by expected $/visitor): paid Get-Featured packages & awards entries → sponsorships/branded series (YourStory, Upworthy Collaborations) → AdSense auto ads → supporter memberships/donations (GNN, Longreads, NPR, Guardian) → contests with sponsor-funded prizes (Moth, Devpost).
3. **Must-have features**: story cards; trending + Boost voting; Born-Today widget; YouTube video hub; newsletter capture in nav/inline/footer; submit-your-story + nominate forms; Get-Featured multi-step lead form with packages; advertise/media-kit page; contests with rules/criteria/prizes/countdown; support page with frequency toggle, presets, purpose allocation, goal progress, supporter wall; careers/talent page; editorial standards, about, contact, privacy, terms, disclaimer (AdSense trust pages).
