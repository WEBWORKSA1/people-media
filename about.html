About People.Media

Last updated: 23 November 2026

People.Media exists to put real people at the centre of media — not only the famous few, but the founders, creators, teachers, caregivers and neighbours whose stories rarely get told.

What we do

  • Original stories and guides on storytelling, personal brand, the creator economy and community.
  • People profiles — fact-checked profiles of remarkable lives and a growing directory of everyday changemakers.
  • Video — curated talks and human stories, refreshed automatically.
  • Contests and the People.Media 100 — giving anyone a path to be published and recognized.
  • Get Featured — professionally written, clearly labelled profiles for founders, creators and professionals.

How we're funded

We are independent. Our revenue comes from display advertising (Google AdSense), clearly labelled sponsorships and partner features, and reader support. Advertisers and sponsors never decide what we cover editorially. See our Editorial Policy.

Our values

  • Consent first. We tell stories with people, not about them.
  • Accuracy. We verify facts and correct mistakes openly.
  • Dignity. No poverty porn, no clickbait at someone's expense.
  • Transparency. Paid content is always labelled.

Get in touch

Use our contact form. For enquiries about this website, the domain name, sponsorship, advertising or partnership, contact our business team.
