Judging criteria

  • Truth & specificity (30%) — real, vivid, verifiable
  • Story craft (30%) — moment, obstacle, change
  • Emotional impact (25%) — does it stay with you?
  • Theme fit (15%)

Shortlists are reviewed by People.Media editors and guest judges. Community Choice votes are reviewed for fraud before results are announced.

Rules in brief

  • Open worldwide to individuals 18+ (under-18s may enter with guardian consent in Youth categories when announced).
  • One entry per person per month. Must be your own original work and a true story.
  • You keep copyright; you grant People.Media a non-exclusive licence to publish and promote your entry.
  • No purchase necessary. Void where prohibited. Prizes are paid within 30 days of announcement.
  • Full terms are in our Terms of Use.
