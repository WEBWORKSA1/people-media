Applying is free. We reply within 2 business days with a feature plan and payment link only if we're a good fit.

Is a paid feature an advertisement?

Paid features are clearly labelled "Partner Feature" in line with advertising disclosure standards. Editorial independence is kept: we fact-check and may decline stories that are misleading.

Can I review before publishing?

Yes — you review for factual accuracy. Our editors keep final say over style and structure.

What if I'm not accepted?

You pay nothing. We'll explain why and suggest a free submission or a better-fit package.

How long does the page stay live?

Permanently, as long as People.Media operates, unless you ask us to remove it.

Can my company pay and get an invoice?

Yes. We provide invoices for businesses on request.
