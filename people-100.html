Methodology

Editors review every nomination against four criteria: measurable impact, evidence, originality, and potential for the recognition to unlock further good. Finalists are interviewed and fact-checked. Sponsors never influence selection.

Honorees receive a published profile, the People.Media 100 badge and an invitation to our virtual celebration.
