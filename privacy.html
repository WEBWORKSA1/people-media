Privacy & Cookie Policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

This policy explains what information People.Media ("we") collects, how we use it and your choices.

Information you give us

When you submit a form (newsletter, story submission, nomination, contest entry, feature application, donation pledge, job application or contact form) we receive the information you enter, such as your name, email address and message. Form submissions are delivered to our team by a third-party form processing service (FormSubmit). We use this information only to respond to you and provide the service you requested.

Information collected automatically

Like most websites, our hosting provider may log technical data such as IP address, browser type and pages visited for security and performance. We store a few preferences in your browser's local storage (theme, cookie choice, boosts you've made, newsletter prompt timing). These never leave your device.

Advertising — Google AdSense

We use Google AdSense to display ads. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to users based on their visit to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.

You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. You can also opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalized advertising at aboutads.info. Learn more about how Google uses information from sites that use its services.

Embedded content

Videos are embedded using YouTube's privacy-enhanced mode (youtube-nocookie.com) and load only when you press play. Our "Born today" widget requests public data from Wikipedia's API. Fonts are served by Google Fonts.

Cookies and your choices

On your first visit we ask for your cookie preference. Choosing "Essential only" requests non-personalized ads. Visitors in the EEA, UK and Switzerland may also be shown Google's certified consent message. You can clear cookies and local storage in your browser at any time.

Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with service providers needed to operate the site (form delivery, hosting, email) or when required by law.

Retention

We keep submissions only as long as needed for the purpose you provided them, or as required by law. You may ask us to delete your data at any time.

Children

People.Media is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal data.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under GDPR, UK GDPR, CCPA/CPRA or India's DPDP Act), you may have rights to access, correct, delete or restrict use of your data. Contact us through our contact form and choose "Removal / privacy request".

Changes

We may update this policy and will change the "last updated" date above.
